## Versioning

M.O.N.I.C.A. follows semantic versioning where practical.

```text
MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH
```

Example:

```text
1.0.0
1.1.0
1.1.1
```

### MAJOR

Breaking changes that may require users or developers to modify their setup.

### MINOR

New backward-compatible features.

### PATCH

Bug fixes, security fixes, and small improvements.

## Changelog Guidelines

When adding a new entry:

* Keep descriptions short and clear.
* Add changes under the appropriate section.
* Avoid exposing credentials or private information.
* Mention breaking changes clearly.
* Keep entries focused on user-visible or developer-relevant changes.
